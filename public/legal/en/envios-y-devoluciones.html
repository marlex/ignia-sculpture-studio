Shipping and Returns Policy

Last updated: June 2026

When you confirm a purchase on Ignia, we take care of coordinating everything that happens afterwards. We manage the collection of the work from the artist's studio, its packaging with specialized materials and the transport to your door through our logistics partners specialized in art. You do not have to manage anything.

Shipping

How it works

Ignia coordinates the collection, packaging and transport of each work through its logistics partners specialized in art, regardless of the size, weight or material of the sculpture. The artist does not manage packaging or shipping under any circumstances.

Each work arrives with the blockchain authenticity certificate, the technical data sheet of the piece and the conservation instructions, with a uniform presentation that meets the Ignia standard.

Ignia acts as the transport organizer, coordinating the contracting of logistics services with specialized operators. The physical performance of the transport and the coverage of the transit insurance are the responsibility of the contracted logistics partners and insurers, under the terms of their respective contracts and policies. In the event of an incident, Ignia facilitates the claim management, but liability for damages caused during transport lies with the corresponding operator and insurer.

Cost transparency

Before confirming the purchase you will see the price of the work, the cost of shipping and insurance broken down, and the total. The shipping cost is calculated automatically based on the destination and the characteristics of the work. No surprises or hidden costs.

Delivery zones and indicative timeframes

Green Zone

Mainland Spain, Portugal, France, Germany, Italy, Belgium, Netherlands

Indicative timeframe: 5 to 14 working days.

Orange Zone

United Kingdom, Switzerland, Middle East, United States, Canada, Mexico

Indicative timeframe: 10 to 21 working days. Ignia manages the coordination of customs procedures through its logistics partners. You do not have to do anything.

Red Zone

Asia, Australia, South America and destinations with cultural export restrictions

Variable timeframe. Ignia will contact you before activating the shipment to confirm the process and applicable conditions.

Custom quote

Large-format works, exceptional weight or value above €30,000

Personalized quote within a maximum of 24 hours after purchase.

Timeframes are indicative and may be affected by external factors beyond Ignia's control, such as weather conditions, holiday periods, customs delays or unforeseen circumstances in transport. Ignia will keep the buyer informed of the shipment status to the extent that the logistics partners provide such information.

Transit insurance

All works travel with all-risk transit insurance, contracted by Ignia through its specialized art insurer, covering total loss, damage and theft during the door-to-door journey. The coverage is based on the declared value of the work.

In the event of an incident, Ignia facilitates the claim management with the insurer. The resolution of the claim and the payment of compensation correspond to the contracted insurer, in accordance with the terms of the policy. The buyer does not have to carry out any procedure directly.

International shipments and customs

For shipments outside the European Union, Ignia coordinates customs procedures through its logistics partners. Original works of art generally benefit from reduced or zero tariff rates in the main markets, although the customs conditions in force at the time of shipment may vary. Any applicable customs costs according to the destination are included in the shipping calculation shown before confirming the purchase, to the extent that such costs are foreseeable. Ignia does not assume liability for delays or additional customs costs derived from regulations of the destination country that were not foreseeable at the time of purchase.

Returns

Damaged work or work that does not match the description

If the work arrives in a condition different from that described in its product file or has been damaged during transport, you have 14 calendar days from receipt to notify us at hola@igniagallery.com or from your customer area, attaching photographs of the condition of the work and the packaging. It is important to keep the original packaging to facilitate the assessment of the incident.

Ignia coordinates the return fully at its own expense. You will receive a full refund of the amount paid (price of the work plus original shipping cost) within a maximum of 14 days from confirmation of the incident, using the same payment method used for the purchase, in accordance with Article 107.1 of Royal Legislative Decree 1/2007.

Change of mind (right of withdrawal)

You have the right to return the work within 14 calendar days from receipt without needing to give any explanation, in accordance with Article 102 of Royal Legislative Decree 1/2007 and Directive 2011/83/EU. The cost of the return is borne by you, although Ignia coordinates the collection through its logistics partners to facilitate the process.

You will receive a refund of the price of the work (not the original shipping cost, in accordance with Article 107.2 TRLGDCU) within a maximum of 14 days from when we receive the work and verify that it is in the same condition in which it was sent.

General conditions

Returns are not accepted for works installed, modified or damaged after receipt due to the buyer's fault. Custom-made works are not subject to the right of withdrawal in accordance with Article 103.c) of Royal Legislative Decree 1/2007 and are expressly identified in their product file.

Ignia acts as an intermediation platform and transport organizer. Its liability in the shipping process is limited to the diligent coordination of the logistics partners and the insurance coverage. Damages caused during transport by causes attributable to the logistics operator or the insurer will be managed through the mechanisms provided for in their respective contracts and policies, without Ignia assuming direct liability for such damages beyond its obligation to facilitate the claim management.

To start any return, write to us at hola@igniagallery.com or contact us from your customer area. We will guide you through the process.
