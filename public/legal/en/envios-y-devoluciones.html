Shipping and Returns Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains, in clear terms, how shipping works for a work purchased on Ignia and what your return rights are. It complements the provisions set out in the Terms and Conditions of Use (sections 3.4 and 4.1 to 4.4), which shall prevail in case of discrepancy.

Shipping

Ignia coordinates the collection of the work from the artist's studio or home, its packaging, and its transport to the address you provide. You do not have to manage any shipping procedure: we take care of contracting the shipping service and the corresponding insurance, and we show you the cost before confirming the purchase.

The cost is calculated according to the size, weight, and fragility of the work: for standard-size and weight pieces, the amount is obtained through courier comparators such as Packlink PRO, which consult the rates of carriers such as Correos, SEUR, or GLS in real time. For large, heavy, or particularly fragile sculptures, shipping is quoted through ARTA, a specialized art logistics platform that works with specific carriers for artwork packaging, crating, and transport; in these cases, while we do not yet have automatic quoting integrated into the platform, we confirm the exact cost within a maximum of 24 hours before you complete payment.

We are in the launch phase and do not yet have stable agreements with specialized art logistics operators beyond these quoting platforms. In the meantime, we always contract insurance coverage proportional to the declared value of the work, either through the carrier or a specific policy: we never ship a work without insurance coverage.

The estimated delivery time depends on the origin, destination, dimensions of the work, and the transport service used in each case, and is shown to you before payment. It does not constitute a guarantee of delivery on an exact date, unless expressly confirmed in your order. We will publish reference delivery times by destination as we consolidate shipping volume.

For shipments outside the European Union, the destination customs may require payment of import duties or import VAT, which are not included in the price shown on the platform and are the responsibility of the buyer.

Returns for damaged or non-conforming works

If the work arrives damaged or does not match the published description, you have 14 calendar days from receipt to notify us at hello@igniagallery.com, attaching photographs of the condition of the work and the packaging. Ignia will refund the full amount paid, including the original shipping cost, within a maximum of 14 days from confirmation of the incident. The collection and return cost are managed and borne by Ignia.

Return due to change of mind (right of withdrawal)

If you purchase as a consumer, you have the right to withdraw from the purchase within 14 calendar days from receipt of the work, without needing to provide any reason, unless it is a work made to a personal commission (which is expressly indicated on the work's page).

In this case, Ignia will refund the price of the work, but not the original shipping cost. The cost of the return is borne by you, although we coordinate the collection to make the process easier. The refund is made within a maximum of 14 days from when we receive and verify the condition of the work.

Returns are not accepted for works installed, modified, or damaged after receipt due to a cause attributable to you, nor for works made to a personal commission.

Summary of timeframes

Notification of damage or non-conformity: 14 calendar days from receipt.

Exercise of the right of withdrawal: 14 calendar days from receipt.

Refund after confirming the incident or receiving the return: maximum 14 days.

Questions about your shipment?

Write to us at hello@igniagallery.com indicating your order number. We will respond with the specific information about your shipment or return.
