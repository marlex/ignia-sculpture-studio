Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies Ignia Gallery ("Ignia") uses on igniagallery.com, for what purpose, and how you can manage them, in compliance with Article 22.2 of Law 34/2002, of 11 July, on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (LSSI-CE) and the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) Cookie Use Guide.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file that a website stores on your device when you visit it. It allows the platform to remember information about your visit, such as your preferences or whether you are logged in, or to perform technical functions necessary for its operation.

2. Cookies currently used by Ignia

At this time, Ignia only uses technical or necessary cookies. This type of cookie is exempt from the obligation to obtain your prior consent, in accordance with Article 22.2 LSSI-CE, because they are strictly necessary for the platform to function or to provide the service you expressly request:

  • Session and authentication cookies: keep you identified while you browse the platform, enable login, and protect your account against unauthorized access.
  • Security and load-balancing cookies from Netlify, Inc., our hosting provider: ensure the correct and secure delivery of pages.
  • Cookies from Stripe, Inc., our payment processing provider: are activated only at the time of completing a purchase and are necessary to process the payment securely and prevent fraud.

None of these cookies are used for analytics, advertising, or profiling purposes, and none are shared with third parties for purposes other than those indicated.

3. Analytics cookies: Google Analytics

Ignia uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to obtain aggregated statistics on platform usage (pages visited, browsing time, traffic source). Unlike technical cookies, these cookies are not necessary for the platform to function and therefore require your prior consent under Article 22.2 LSSI-CE:

  • _ga: anonymously identifies your browser to distinguish unique users. Duration: up to 2 years.
  • _ga_<property identifier>: maintains session state for the same purpose. Duration: up to 2 years.

These cookies are only installed if you accept the "Analytics" category in the cookie settings panel; if you reject it, they are not activated. Google LLC acts as a data processor and is adherent to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which supports the transfer of this data to the United States in accordance with the European Commission's Adequacy Decision of 10 July 2023. More information about this processing can be found in the Privacy Policy.

4. Other cookies we do not currently use

Ignia does not use personalization or advertising cookies (for example, Meta or Google Ads pixels). If we incorporate any of these technologies in the future, we will update this Cookie Policy to describe them and activate the corresponding category in the settings panel so that you can give or deny your consent before any new cookie is installed.

5. How to manage cookies

When you first enter igniagallery.com, a panel allows you to accept all cookies, reject all non-essential cookies, or configure your choice category by category, with the same ease to accept as to reject. The technical cookies described in section 2 are always installed, as they are exempt from consent; the analytics cookie described in section 3 is only installed if you expressly accept it. You can change your choice at any time from the settings panel itself.

Regardless of the panel, you can delete or block cookies at any time from your browser settings. Please note that blocking technical cookies may prevent the platform from functioning correctly, including logging in or making purchases.

6. Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in the cookies we use or in applicable legislation. We recommend that you consult it periodically.
