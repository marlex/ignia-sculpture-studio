3.3 Commissions and editorial levels

Ignia applies a commission on the sale price of each work according to the editorial level assigned to the artist within the platform. The level is determined by Ignia using objective, public and verifiable criteria, applied uniformly to all artists regardless of gender, geographic origin or prior commercial trajectory.

Emerging — 18%

Artists in the early phases of their public career. No exhibition history or previous sales are required. The entry criterion is work produced with consistency and sufficient editorial quality to be on Ignia. Founding artists of the platform have guaranteed permanent and free access to all tools of the upper level without conditions or expiry date, as structural recognition for those who built Ignia from the beginning.

Established — 15%

Artists with a demonstrable public career. They must meet at least two of the following criteria: individual or group exhibitions in venues with public programming, work in documented private or institutional collections, presence in specialized media or art publications, or documented previous sales above €3,000 per piece.

Featured — 12%

Artists with a consolidated career and sector recognition. They must meet at least three of the following criteria: active representation by a gallery, work in institutional collections or museums, participation in international reference fairs, documented secondary market price, or editorial coverage in national or international art media.

The level may be updated as the artist's career evolves, following a reasoned request by the artist or periodic review by Ignia. The criteria for assignment and updating of level are public and available on the platform at all times. Payment to the artist is made within 14 calendar days of confirmation of delivery of the work to the buyer. In case of return due to the buyer's withdrawal, Ignia does not retain commission on that transaction.

The artist who subscribes to the optional Ignia Pro plan (€9.90/month) gains access to advanced audience analytics tools, preferential positioning, up to 20 simultaneous active works and priority access to new features. The Pro plan does not modify the editorial level or the applicable commission, which is determined exclusively by the level assigned according to the criteria above.

4.3 Returns policy

If the work arrives damaged or does not match the published description, the buyer has 14 calendar days from receipt to notify it, attaching photographs of the condition of the work and the packaging. Ignia will refund the full amount paid, including the original shipping, within a maximum of 14 days from confirmation of the incident. The return is fully managed and financed by Ignia.

If the buyer exercises the right of withdrawal due to a change of mind within 14 calendar days, Ignia will refund the price of the work. The cost of the original shipping is not refunded, in accordance with Article 107.2 TRLGDCU. The cost of the return is borne by the buyer, although Ignia coordinates the collection to facilitate the process. The refund is made within a maximum of 14 days from receipt and verification of the condition of the work.

Returns are not accepted for works installed, modified or damaged after receipt due to the buyer's fault, nor for custom-made works.
