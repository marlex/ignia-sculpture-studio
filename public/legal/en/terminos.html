3.3 Commissions and editorial levels

Ignia applies a commission on the sale price of each work according to the editorial level assigned to the artist within the platform. The level is determined by Ignia using objective, public and verifiable criteria, applied uniformly to all artists regardless of gender, geographic origin or prior commercial trajectory.

Emerging — 18%

Artists in the early phases of their public career. No exhibition history or previous sales are required. The entry criterion is work produced with consistency and sufficient editorial quality to be on Ignia. Founding artists of the platform have guaranteed permanent and free access to all tools of the upper level without conditions or expiry date, as structural recognition for those who built Ignia from the beginning. This benefit is personal and non-transferable, and may be revoked by Ignia in case of fraud, serious breach of these terms or abusive use of the platform by the artist.

Established — 15%

Artists with a demonstrable public career. They must meet at least two of the following criteria: individual or group exhibitions in venues with public programming, work in documented private or institutional collections, presence in specialized media or art publications, or documented previous sales above €3,000 per piece.

Featured — 12%

Artists with a consolidated career and sector recognition. They must meet at least three of the following criteria: active representation by a gallery, work in institutional collections or museums, participation in international reference fairs, documented secondary market price, or editorial coverage in national or international art media.

The level may be updated as the artist's career evolves, following a reasoned request by the artist or periodic review by Ignia. The criteria for assignment and updating of level are public and available on the platform at all times.

Payment to the artist is made within 14 calendar days of confirmation of delivery of the work to the buyer. In case of return due to the buyer's withdrawal, Ignia does not retain commission on that transaction.

Ignia may withhold payment, in whole or in part, while a dispute, claim, reasonable suspicion of fraud or a chargeback procedure initiated by the buyer's payment institution is in progress, until the incident is resolved. If the payment has already been released to the artist and a chargeback, a cancellation of the payment by the issuing institution or a confirmed fraud subsequently occurs, Ignia may claim from the artist the return of the amounts unduly received or offset them against future payments due for other sales.

The artist who subscribes to the optional Ignia Pro plan (€9.90/month) gains access to advanced audience analytics tools, preferential positioning, up to 20 simultaneous active works and priority access to new features. The Pro plan does not modify the editorial level or the applicable commission, which is determined exclusively by the level assigned according to the criteria above.

Under this regulation, Ignia must collect from each artist identifying information (full name or company name, address, tax identification number or equivalent, and payment account data) and report it annually to the Spanish Tax Agency via form 238, unless the artist qualifies as an "excluded seller" for carrying out fewer than 30 transactions and receiving a total amount below €2,000 in the calendar year. Consequently, Ignia may require the buyer and the artist, prior to or during the processing of a transaction of that amount or greater, additional identifying documentation and, where appropriate, information on the origin of the funds, being able to suspend or reject the operation until such verification is completed.
