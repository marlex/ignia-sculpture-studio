Accessibility

Ignia Institution accessibility statement and commitment

Last updated: July 2026

Our commitment

At Ignia, we work to make discovering, exploring, and buying sculpture by emerging artists an accessible experience for as many people as possible, including people with disabilities. Accessibility is an ongoing process: we apply good design practices from the development of the platform and continue to advance progressively as the project grows.

About the platform

Ignia is an online platform that allows you to:

  • Explore and discover sculpture works and artist profiles.
  • View each work's details, including 3D visualization.
  • Register, log in, and save works of interest.
  • Buy a work and complete payment securely.

The platform is built on modern web technologies that incorporate accessibility best practices by default, such as semantic content structure and general keyboard navigation support. We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as a reference to guide platform improvements, and we will incorporate more formal verifications as the project matures.

Regulatory situation and scope of this statement

Since June 28, 2025, Law 11/2023, of May 8, on the accessibility of certain products and services (which transposes Directive (EU) 2019/882, European Accessibility Act) requires accessibility requirements for e-commerce services directed at consumers. Its Article 3 recognizes an exemption for microenterprises (fewer than 10 employees and less than 2 million euros in annual turnover or balance sheet), a condition that currently applies to Ignia.

This statement voluntarily and transparently describes Ignia's approach to accessibility and the channel enabled to report any usability difficulty.

Continuous improvement

The platform's accessibility is reviewed progressively as new functionalities are added. Some components, such as the 3D artwork viewer, present greater technical complexity for adaptation to assistive technologies such as screen readers, and are part of the areas in which we continue to work.

How to report an accessibility barrier

If you encounter an accessibility barrier while using igniagallery.com, or need information about a work in an alternative format, write to us at hello@igniagallery.com describing the problem and the page where it occurs. Our goal is to respond within a maximum of 10 business days.

Complaints procedure

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the market surveillance authority designated by the Community of Madrid under Chapter XI of Law 11/2023, or the corresponding surveillance authority in your autonomous community of residence.

Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared in July 2026 through an internal evaluation by the Ignia team. It will be reviewed and updated periodically as the platform evolves and new accessibility improvements are incorporated.
