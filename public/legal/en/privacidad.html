Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ignia Gallery ("Ignia", "we") collects, uses, retains and protects the personal data of artists, buyers and visitors of the platform, in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of 27 April 2016 ("GDPR") and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 of 5 December on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights ("LOPDGDD").

1. Data controller

The data controller is Mariana Bolívar García, a natural person and holder of the Ignia Gallery project, with a communications address in Madrid, Spain. Contact: privacidad@igniagallery.com. Full identification details are available in the Legal Notice.

2. Data we collect

We collect different categories of data depending on your relationship with the platform:

Visitors: browsing and platform usage data (see the Cookie Policy for details on cookies and similar technologies).

Artists: full name, email address, country, portfolio and artwork images, bank details for commission payments, and, for transactions of €10,000 or more, additional identification documents (ID/passport, tax number) in accordance with section 10.3 of the Terms and Conditions of Use (anti-money laundering regulations).

Buyers: full name, email address, delivery address, payment data (processed directly by Stripe; Ignia does not store card numbers), and, for transactions of €10,000 or more, additional identification documents in accordance with the same section.

Data derived from platform activity: history of works viewed, saved or purchased, interactions with the 3D viewer, and communications with customer support.

3. Purposes and legal basis of processing

We process your personal data for the following purposes, each with its legal basis under Article 6.1 GDPR:

Managing your account, publishing works and processing purchases: performance of a contract (Article 6.1.b GDPR).

Generating the blockchain certificate of authenticity and transferring it between owners: performance of a contract, with the specific processing described in section 4.

Managing logistics, transit insurance and returns: performance of a contract.

Complying with tax obligations, reporting to the Spanish Tax Agency (DAC7, Law 13/2023) and, where applicable, anti-money laundering (Law 10/2010): compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6.1.c GDPR).

Preventing fraud, verifying identities in high-value transactions and protecting the security of the platform: legitimate interest (Article 6.1.f GDPR), balanced against your rights and interests.

Sending you commercial communications or the newsletter: consent (Article 6.1.a GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Blockchain certificate: specific processing

The certificate of authenticity is generated through Verisart, which uses the OpenTimestamps protocol on the Bitcoin network. This protocol does not record your personal data directly on the blockchain: what is permanently anchored on the network is a cryptographic hash which, on its own, does not allow you to be identified or the original information to be reconstructed. The descriptive data of the artwork and artist (name, images, technical data) are stored on Verisart's systems, off-chain, where they can indeed be rectified or deleted in accordance with that provider's procedures.

This architecture is consistent with the Guidelines on the processing of personal data through blockchain technologies approved by the European Data Protection Board, for which the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) served as rapporteur, which recommend avoiding the recording of plain-text personal data on the chain and limiting on-chain anchoring to hashes or cryptographic commitments. Ignia has a data processor agreement with Verisart that reflects these safeguards.

You should be aware that, although descriptive data may be rectified or deleted from Verisart's systems, the corresponding cryptographic hash will remain indefinitely and publicly on the Bitcoin network as proof that such information existed and was declared on a given date. This permanence is an essential and necessary feature of the service that you accept when publishing a work or receiving a certificate as a buyer.

We share your data, only to the extent necessary, with the following processors or third parties:

Competent authorities (including SEPBLAC), where required under anti-money laundering regulations or upon legal request.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties nor use it for purposes other than those described in this policy.

6. International transfers

Some of our providers are located outside the European Economic Area. Stripe, Inc. and Netlify, Inc. are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, recognised as providing an adequate level of protection by the European Commission's Adequacy Decision of 10 July 2023, allowing data transfers without additional safeguards. For Verisart, Inc., given the limited nature of the data that actually crosses the border pursuant to section 4, Ignia obtains the appropriate safeguards (adherence to the aforementioned framework or Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission) in its data processor agreement, and keeps them available for consultation at the request of the data subject.

7. Retention periods

We retain your data during the term of your account and, after its cancellation, for the periods legally required: as a general rule, data linked to tax obligations is retained for 4 years pursuant to Article 66 of Law 58/2003 (General Tax Law) and accounting documents for 6 years pursuant to Article 30 of the Commercial Code; identification data collected for compliance with anti-money laundering regulations is retained for 10 years pursuant to Article 25 of Law 10/2010; and data reported to the Tax Agency under DAC7 is retained for the periods established by that regulation. The cryptographic hash of the blockchain certificate remains indefinitely on the Bitcoin network, as explained in section 4.

8. Your rights

You may exercise at any time the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability of your data, as well as withdraw your consent where processing is based on it, by writing to privacidad@igniagallery.com. We will respond within one month in accordance with Article 12.3 GDPR. The rights of rectification and erasure over the descriptive data of your blockchain certificate are exercised against Verisart through Ignia, with the limits explained in section 4 regarding the cryptographic hash anchored on the Bitcoin network.

If you consider that the processing of your data does not comply with the applicable regulations, you have the right to file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD), through its electronic office at www.aepd.es.

9. Minors

Access to Ignia is restricted to persons over 18 years of age or minors with authorisation from their legal representative, in accordance with the Terms and Conditions of Use. We do not knowingly collect data from minors without such authorisation.

10. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration, in accordance with Article 32 GDPR. Payment processing is carried out through Stripe, which is PCI-DSS certified; Ignia does not at any time store full payment card data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect regulatory changes or changes in our services. Relevant changes will be notified to registered users with a minimum of 15 days' notice, as provided for in the Terms and Conditions of Use.
