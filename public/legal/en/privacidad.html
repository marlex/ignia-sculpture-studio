Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Data controller: Ignia Gallery, a project in the process of incorporation as a company in Spain. Provisional controller: Mariana Bolívar García, Madrid. Contact: privacidad@igniagallery.com.

Supervisory authority: Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD), www.aepd.es. Legal basis: Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (GDPR) and Organic Law 3/2018, of 5 December, on Personal Data Protection and guarantee of digital rights (LOPDGDD).

At Ignia Gallery we treat privacy as a commitment, not a formality. This policy explains what data we collect, what we use it for and what rights you have over it, in a clear way and without unnecessary technicalities.

1. What data we collect and why

Account and registration data

Name, surname, email address and password. Purpose: to manage your access to the platform and the contractual relationship. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR). Retention period: while the account is active and for an additional 5 years for applicable legal obligations.

Additional artist data

Profile photograph, biographical description, portfolio, bank details for payment of sales, country of residence and assigned editorial level. Purpose: to manage the public profile, process payments, issue blockchain certificates and apply the corresponding commission level. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).

Additional buyer data

Delivery address, contact details and payment data. Card data is managed directly by the payment provider (Stripe) under PCI-DSS standards. Ignia does not store card data at any time. Purpose: to manage the purchase, the shipping logistics and after-sales service. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR). Transaction data is retained for 10 years for tax and accounting obligations.

Browsing data

IP address, device and browser type, pages visited and session duration. Purpose: to ensure the technical operation of the platform and to analyse use in an aggregated way to improve the service. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR), balanced against users' rights. Retention period: 13 months.

Audience analytics data for artists

Aggregated and anonymized data about the behaviour of visitors who interact with the works: country of origin, device type, time spent in the 3D viewer and visit frequency. This data does not allow individual visitors to be identified and is always presented in an aggregated form. Purpose: to offer the artist audience information about their work to improve their sales strategy. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

Data associated with the blockchain certificate

When publishing a work, the artist declares certain data (title, authorship, materials, dimensions, date) that are immutably recorded on the blockchain through Verisart. Once recorded, these data cannot be modified or deleted from the blockchain record due to the technological nature of the system. The artist expressly accepts this characteristic when publishing their work. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).

Communications

Messages you send us by email or through the platform. Purpose: to attend to queries and complaints. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR). Retention period: 3 years.

2. How long we keep your data

Account data is kept while you maintain an active account on Ignia and for the minimum period required by applicable legislation once you cancel it. Transaction data is kept for 10 years for tax and accounting obligations derived from the Spanish Commercial Code and tax legislation. Browsing data is kept for 13 months. When you request deletion of your account, Ignia will delete your personal data within a maximum period of 30 days, except for data that must be kept by legal obligation or that forms part of a blockchain record, whose technological nature prevents its modification or deletion.

3. Who we share your data with

Ignia shares data exclusively with the third parties necessary to provide the service, as data processors or independent controllers as appropriate:

With Stripe, to process transactions securely under PCI-DSS standards, as an independent controller of payment data. With logistics partners (EnviArte, SIT Spain or other art specialists), the buyer's full name and delivery address and the artist's collection address, exclusively to manage the shipping of each work. With Verisart, the declared data of each work for the issuance of the blockchain authenticity certificate. With the specialized art insurer, the work data and contact details of artist and buyer, exclusively in the event of a claim during transit. With Netlify, the hosting infrastructure provider acting as a data processor and unable to use your data for its own purposes.

Ignia does not sell, rent or transfer personal data to third parties for commercial or advertising purposes. If any of these providers is located outside the European Economic Area, Ignia ensures that the processing is carried out with appropriate safeguards in accordance with Chapter V of the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or other equivalent mechanisms approved by the European Commission).

4. Your rights

You may exercise any of the following rights by writing to privacidad@igniagallery.com, stating your full name and attaching a copy of your identification document. We will respond within a maximum period of one month, extendable by two additional months in cases of special complexity, with prior notification.

Access

Know what data of yours we process and obtain a copy.

Rectification

Correct inaccurate data or complete incomplete data.

Erasure

Request the deletion of your data when it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, except for legal retention obligations or data already recorded on the blockchain.

Objection

Object to processing based on legitimate interest.

Restriction

Request that we restrict the use of your data in certain circumstances.

Portability

Receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.

If you consider that the processing of your data is not correct or that we have not dealt with your request satisfactorily, you may file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at www.aepd.es.

5. Cookies

Ignia uses strictly necessary technical cookies for the operation of the platform (session management, language preferences, shopping cart). These cookies do not require consent in accordance with Article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE, interpreted in light of the AEPD guidelines.

Ignia may use analytics cookies to understand aggregated use of the platform and improve the service. In that case, we will inform you and request your consent through the cookie banner before activating them. You may withdraw your consent at any time from the platform's cookie settings.

Ignia does not use advertising cookies or share browsing data with third-party advertising networks.

6. Security

All communications between your browser and the platform are carried out using the HTTPS protocol with TLS encryption. Payment data is managed entirely through Stripe under PCI-DSS standards, without Ignia storing card data at any time. Ignia applies appropriate technical and organizational measures —including access control, encryption of sensitive data and security monitoring— to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, accidental or unlawful alteration or destruction, in accordance with Article 32 of the GDPR.

In the event of a security breach that may affect your data, Ignia will notify the AEPD within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and will inform you if such breach entails a high risk for your rights and freedoms, in accordance with Articles 33 and 34 of the GDPR.

7. Changes to this policy

When we update this policy we will notify you by email at least 15 days in advance if the changes significantly affect the processing of your data. The current version, with its date of last update, will always be available on this same page.
